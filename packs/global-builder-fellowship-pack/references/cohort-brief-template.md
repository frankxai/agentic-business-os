## Six-week cell rhythm

| Week | Decision question | Evidence artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What is safe, useful, and bounded? | Build contract |
| 2 | What is the smallest proof? | Prototype / research record |
| 3 | What happens at the edges? | Evaluation cases |
| 4 | What did someone close to the problem learn? | Feedback evidence |
| 5 | Continue, redirect, pause, or refer? | Decision record |
| 6 | What is private, public, or opt-in? | Proof packet |
