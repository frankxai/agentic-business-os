# Global Builder Fellowship

Use this pack to design a small, locally stewarded builder fellowship. Its job
is to create real opportunity for participants—not to harvest ideas, source
cheap labor, or promise funding.

## Preconditions

Before proposing a challenge, collect only the information that changes safety
or program design:

- community / country and the local steward's role, compensation, and conflicts;
- participant age range, language, accessibility, timezone, and connectivity
  needs;
- one locally understood problem and the people who experience it;
- participant value: support, tools, time, feedback, and realistic next paths;
- budget status and who can authorize payments;
- data, safeguarding, IP, media, and withdrawal boundaries;
- supporter / sponsor interests and explicit limits on their access;
- what a good result looks like even if no startup is funded.

If there is no trusted local steward, a clear data/IP/payment route, or a
participant benefit that does not depend on winning, stop at a discovery memo.
Do not draft a public challenge yet.

## Workflow

1. **Start with local listening.** Identify the people who understand the
   problem. Frame a question with them; do not import a fashionable problem.
2. **Write one bounded challenge.** Ask for a problem, smallest proof, and
   learning plan—not a comprehensive business plan or polished pitch deck.
3. **Set participant ownership.** Default to participant ownership of project
   IP. Separate any sponsor, customer, employment, co-creation, or venture deal
   into later terms.
4. **Make access real.** Specify language, schedule, disability, connectivity,
   caregiving, and cost barriers. A global form in one language is not an
   accessibility plan.
5. **Use fair selection.** Use the rubric in
   [references/fair-selection-rubric.md](references/fair-selection-rubric.md).
   Blind the first round where practical and use at least two independent,
   conflict-disclosed reviewers.
6. **Run proof cells.** Form small groups with a steward. Require one testable
   artifact and expected, edge, and blocked evaluation cases each week.
7. **Separate community from capital.** Alumni support is the default. Mentor,
   customer, sponsor, and investor access is opt-in and never a condition of
   selection or funding guarantee.
8. **Write the stop rules.** No cohort launches without local stewardship,
   participant terms, payment/safeguarding review, reviewer capacity, and an
   escalation route.
9. **Return a complete brief.** Use
   [references/cohort-brief-template.md](references/cohort-brief-template.md).

## Quality gate

The proposal must include:

- a compensated, conflict-disclosed local steward;
- a problem co-authored or materially approved by people close to it;
- a participant benefit independent of selection;
- participant-first IP, data, media, and withdrawal defaults;
- an accessibility and language plan;
- a blind-first-round, two-reviewer selection process;
- stipend/prize language only when a real, approved payment route exists;
- an explicit sponsor and supporter access boundary;
- a six-week-or-shorter build cadence with evidence artifacts;
- appeal, incident, and pause/exit routes;
- metrics about fairness and proof quality, not just applications or headlines;
- clearly labeled facts, assumptions, hypotheses, and human approvals.

## Refuse or redesign

- a country or community framed as low-cost labor;
- unpaid work for a sponsor, client, investor, or operator as a condition of
  participation;
- mandatory IP assignment, perpetual publicity, or disclosure of a private
  prototype;
- an AI ranker that decides admission or infers merit from proxy data;
- public investor solicitation, fee-for-introduction, pooled capital, or a
  promise of investment, job placement, grant, or income;
- a global launch with no local steward, language plan, or payment/safeguarding
  review;
- a showcase that exposes participant data or gives supporters private judging
  influence;
- “impact” claims without participant, problem, and evidence integrity;
- an always-on community with no accountable steward, escalation route, or
  privacy boundary.

## Output

Return these headings:

1. `Program thesis and non-goal`
2. `Local steward and country gate`
3. `Challenge brief`
4. `Participant value and protections`
5. `Accessibility, language, and operations`
6. `Selection rubric and reviewer process`
7. `Six-week proof-cell rhythm`
8. `Community and supporter boundaries`
9. `Measurement and stop rules`
10. `Human approvals required before launch`
11. `One next action`
