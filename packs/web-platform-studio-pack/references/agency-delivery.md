# Agency delivery and repeatable production

The agency practice should improve the reusable product library with each delivery. Keep customer commitments bounded by measured capacity, clear ownership and a working support path.

## End-to-end workflow

| Stage | Deliverable | Decision/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Qualify | Buyer, job, existing system, urgency, budget context, success measure | Suitable platform and feasible scope |
| Discover | Audience research, content/data inventory, accounts, rights, integration map | Explicit assumptions and dependencies |
| Scope | Deliverables, exclusions, milestones, acceptance, revisions, operations | Approved concrete brief and commercial scope |
| Design | Content structure, art direction, tokens, representative screens | Brand-owner direction plus usability critique |
| Build | One full journey, then supporting states/integrations | Functional verification and iterative refinement |
| Verify | Accessibility, performance, permissions, data, search, domain and recovery | Revision-bound evidence; independent review |
| Launch | Approved release packet, migration, domain plan, ownership | Actual endpoint/journey evidence and rollback target |
| Handoff | Source, assets, documentation, accounts, support, training and exit | Second operator can perform an ordinary update |
| Improve | Usage, conversions, support burden, defects and updates | A bounded improvement backlog based on evidence |

For a productized offer, specify page/content scope, integrations, revision rounds, customer-supplied inputs, turnaround dependencies and maintenance terms. Define change requests when the job expands. Do not promise unlimited work or a delivery date dependent on unknown customer inputs.

## Human and agent responsibilities

A product/design lead owns interpretation, taste and final integration. Specialist capabilities can support research, architecture, asset generation, code, search and operations when their bounded contribution is needed. An independent reviewer verifies consequential work. A named human retains authority over brand identity, contract terms, production account ownership and release decisions required by the host.

Store instructions as modular skills: intake, architecture choice, identity production, page assembly, search inspection, domain change proposal, release verification and handoff. Each skill needs inputs, outputs, source hierarchy, tool permissions, stop conditions and examples/evaluation cases. A large list of agents is not itself a delivery system.

## Required business moving parts

- Lead capture and qualification connected to an existing customer system.
- Scope/proposal, acceptance criteria, change requests and customer approvals.
- Contract, deposit/milestones, invoicing and payment reconciliation in the appropriate business systems.
- Content collection and account-access process with minimum necessary data.
- Source ownership, asset rights, third-party licenses and customer portfolio permission.
- Support channel, incident ownership, maintenance scope and response expectations.
- Customer handoff, source/data export, credential revocation and offboarding.
- Offer economics including actual design/build/review/support labor.

Keep legal and commercial templates clearly marked for appropriate review; an AI-generated contract is not an approved legal agreement. Use current local requirements and qualified review for concrete legal obligations.

## Portfolio-to-product extraction

After a delivery, identify what is generic and reusable. Obtain permission for a case study; independently verify any result stated. Extract source patterns and original assets only where rights allow it. Make the extracted template work with a new customer's content and accounts. Record lineage back to the source project without exposing private details.

The case study should explain the job, constraints, our actual role, design/architecture decisions, work delivered, observable outcomes, live status checked on a stated date and next improvements. It can link to an available template or useful tutorial. A live link alone does not prove that the product is maintained or financially successful.

## First operational rehearsal

Choose one owned production site with a known source owner and public permission. Populate the project record, run the primary buyer journey, attach current domain/search/quality evidence, and have a second operator follow the handoff. Fix the gaps that appear before multiplying editions or signing broad service commitments.
