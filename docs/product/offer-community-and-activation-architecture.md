# Offer, community, and activation architecture

> **2026-07-14 update:** the proof-cell and value-first activation principles remain current. Guided/manual sprint and white-label sections are pre-autonomy concepts; paid delivery and licensing now follow the [autonomous product ladder](./autonomous-product-ladder-and-delivery.md) and its no-human-implementation gate.

## Activation thesis

People do not need a larger content library. They need one route, a short time to first proof, and a social contract that rewards implementation.

The product should make the next move obvious without coercion:

- one diagnostic result;
- one artifact to build;
- one approval boundary;
- one Friday proof;
- one next offer after the artifact exists.

## The Pathfinder output

Every Pathfinder run returns:

1. audience route: student, restart, founder, agency, or SME;
2. selected process and buyer;
3. evidence that the process is painful or valuable;
4. smallest proof artifact;
5. architecture route with rejected alternatives;
6. seven-day plan;
7. human gates and stop conditions;
8. one next CTA.

It must never return ten equal ideas. Choice overload is a failure.

## Seven-day proof sprint

| Day | Artifact |
|---|---|
| 1 | Process map, buyer, baseline, and risk boundary |
| 2 | One-process-to-one-offer canvas |
| 3 | Repository, doctrine, agent/skill contract, and test cases |
| 4 | Working local or preview artifact |
| 5 | Demo, evaluation results, and proof packet |
| 6 | Pilot brief, scope, price hypothesis, support boundary |
| 7 | Retrospective, reusable asset extraction, next decision |

## Proof-cell contract

A Foundry Circle cell has 3–5 builders. Each member brings an artifact and one unresolved decision. The weekly ritual is:

1. state the promised proof;
2. show the artifact or receipt;
3. name one failure or surprise;
4. receive one useful review;
5. update business memory;
6. commit the next bounded proof.

No revenue leaderboard, public shame, or fabricated activity. Community claims require real participation evidence.

## Offer boundaries

### Free/open

The open layer must be genuinely usable: Pathfinder, templates, agent contracts, safety gates, and one working example. Open source is distribution and trust, not a crippled trial.

### Paid packs

Paid packs add curation, guided sequences, vertical configuration, evaluation cases, deployment proof, updates, and support—not hidden basics.

### Guided sprint

The sprint sells implementation pressure and expert review. Done means a working system, offer page, proof packet, and pilot plan—not course completion.

### White-label operator

Before sale, the operator license must define:

- commercial and resale rights;
- rebranding and attribution;
- client/install limits;
- included update period;
- support response and exclusions;
- security, privacy, claims, and human-approval obligations;
- proof requirements before public claims;
- termination and downstream update behavior.

Use the existing property implementer model as the reference: template product → implementation business → managed operations.

## Trust gates

- No income, ROI, job-replacement, customer-result, or time-saving guarantee without evidence.
- Separate live product, demo, sample, proposal, and roadmap.
- Case studies need explicit permission.
- Pricing remains labeled as hypothesis until checkout and delivery are verified.
- Every kit carries license, provenance, support boundary, security notes, tests, `lastVerified`, changelog, and deployment evidence.
- Affiliate content carries disclosure and current program verification.
- Always-on agents carry heartbeats, audit logs, least privilege, and human approval boundaries.

## Funnel instrumentation

Track PII-minimized events:

- `pathfinder_started`
- `pathfinder_route_selected`
- `proof_plan_created`
- `starter_fork_clicked`
- `starter_build_verified`
- `preview_deployed`
- `proof_packet_completed`
- `sprint_application_started`
- `sprint_application_submitted`
- `circle_proof_posted`
- `pilot_brief_completed`

Primary metric: median time from Pathfinder start to first verified proof artifact.
